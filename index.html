    How to Play

    Welcome to The Great Quiz Database, your destination for questions across a wide range of categories!

    Test your knowledge, discover new topics, and enjoy an engaging quiz experience with questions for every kind of trivia enthusiast.

    Each time you play, you will receive a random set of questions. Choose a category on the home page to play with questions from that category only, or leave the selection on all categories.

    Save your high score on our leaderboard, then dive back in for another round and aim to beat it!
